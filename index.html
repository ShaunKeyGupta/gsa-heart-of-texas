Is the appraisal really free?

Yes. The appraisal and our cash offer are completely free, with no obligation to sell. You’ll leave knowing what your items are worth either way.

Do I have to sell anything?

Never. Many people come just to learn what they have. If you decide to sell, you can take cash on the spot or consign to auction, your call.

What should I bring?

Whatever you’re curious about, coins, gold, silver, jewelry, watches, firearms, or an entire estate. A photo texted to 254-327-4050 works too if it’s hard to transport.

How does the $50 bonus work?

Sell any single item for $500 or more during fair week (Oct 8-18) and we’ll add a $50 cash bonus on top of your offer.

Are you licensed to buy firearms?

Yes, Gold Standard Auctions is a licensed FFL and bonded dealer. All firearm transactions follow federal and Texas law.
